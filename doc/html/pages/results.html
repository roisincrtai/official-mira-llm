CUDA performance

The manuscript compares parameter-matched modules on an NVIDIA RTX A6000 at 32,768 tokens. The reported MIRA forward-pass time is 11.37 ms; reported maximum-batch throughput is 8.36 million tokens/s. These measure the module, not full-model generation throughput. Latency and maximum-batch throughput use different measurement conditions and should not be converted into each other.
